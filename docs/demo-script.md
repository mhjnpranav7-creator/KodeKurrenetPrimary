# Five-minute demo script

1. **Problem (0:00-0:30):** People can record exercise but do not get understandable movement feedback without equipment.
2. **Product (0:30-1:00):** FormFlow turns a camera into an explainable squat report.
3. **Input (1:00-1:45):** Show camera guidance and choose the prepared sample. Mention that upload and camera capture are also supported.
4. **Analysis (1:45-2:45):** Show pose landmarks, phase, rep count, confidence, and quality flags.
5. **Report (2:45-3:45):** Click a rep, show depth/tempo flags, the timeline, and the chart.
6. **AI (3:45-4:20):** Open the explanation. Emphasize that the AI explains measured values; it does not count from a vague prompt.
7. **Engineering (4:20-5:00):** MediaPipe runs locally, Fastify validates optional persistence/AI, Supabase is ready for history, and the deterministic fallback keeps the demo working. State clearly that this is wellness feedback, not medical advice.
